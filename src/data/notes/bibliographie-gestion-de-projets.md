## Standards et référentiels

### PMI — Project Management Institute

- *A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide)*, 8ᵉ éd., 2025 ; 7ᵉ éd., 2021.
- *The Standard for Project Management*, 2021.
- *Agile Practice Guide*, 2017.
- *The Standard for Program Management*, 4ᵉ éd., 2017.
- *The Standard for Portfolio Management*, 4ᵉ éd., 2017.
- *The Standard for Earned Value Management*, 2019.

### PRINCE2 et méthodes associées — AXELOS

- *Managing Successful Projects with PRINCE2* (dernière édition).
- *PRINCE2 Agile*.
- *Managing Successful Programmes* (MSP).
- *Management of Portfolios* (MoP).
- *Management of Risk* (M_o_R).

### Normes ISO — série *Project, programme and portfolio management*

- ISO 21500:2021 — *Context and concepts*.
- ISO 21502:2020 — *Guidance on project management*.
- ISO 21503:2022 — *Guidance on programme management*.
- ISO 21504:2022 — *Guidance on portfolio management*.
- ISO 21505:2017 — *Guidance on governance*.
- ISO/TR 21506:2018 — *Vocabulary*.
- ISO 21508:2018 — *Earned value management in project and programme management*.
- ISO 10006 — *Quality management systems — Guidelines for quality management in projects*.

### Autres référentiels

- IPMA — *Individual Competence Baseline* (ICB 4).
- AACE International — *Total Cost Management Framework* et *Recommended Practices* (estimation, maîtrise des coûts, valeur acquise).
- EIA-748 — *Earned Value Management Systems* (norme américaine).
